## 🎮 About the Game

Garden of Witches is a single-player action roguelike developed and published by Team Tapas. It launched in full on August 28, 2026, after entering Early Access on May 23, 2025. Players control Sil, the Thread Witch, as she battles through the Garden, confronts rival witches, and gathers them for an annual tea party ritual.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Garden of Witches supports keyboard and mouse controls with free mouse aiming. Move Sil normally, use the trainer's movement option only when needed, and remember that Rush can function as both a damaging dash and a follow-up ability that pulls enemies connected by thread.

### Tools & Inventory

The game organizes combat equipment through Sil's Armory rather than a traditional item inventory. Choose available Scissors, Spells, and Special Skills before a run. During a run, Imprints and Relics reshape abilities and create new interactions, while Runes provide additional preparation and utility bonuses.

### World & Environment

The adventure begins at Sil's House and continues through chapter-based areas made up of combat rooms and witch encounters. Progress unlocks additional equipment and story content across the main five-chapter arc and three-part epilogue. Challenge Mode becomes available after clearing Epilogue 3.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines giant scissors, witchcraft, and special skills. Abilities may use charging, combos, channeling, Sharpness, Thread, Flower, or Crow effects depending on the selected equipment. Learn each witch's attack patterns, build around compatible Imprints and Relics, and activate trainer options selectively so the run remains stable.
